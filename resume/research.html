Research Experience

Undergraduate Research Assistant - Advanced Vertical Flight Laboratory, College Station TX (August 2026 - Present)

Roughly 5 hours per week, I assist in conducting experiments and analyzing data related to vertical flight dynamics. I have learned how to conduct precise data collection and analysis techniques using LabVIEW. Experiments include a torque cell calibration, dynamometer testing, and a dynamometer experiment.

Torque Cell Calibration

  • Mounted the torque cell on a precision stand with a rod screwed into it.
  • Applied known weights to the end of the rod to generate torque.
  • Utilized LabVIEW to record and analyze the torque cell output data.
  • Recorded the output signal of the torque cell at various load conditions.
  • Established a linear relationship between the applied torque and the output signal.
  • Used the slope of the line as the calibration factor for accurate measurements.

Dynamometer Testing

  • Mounted the primary motor on one end of the dynamometer and the secondary load motor on the other end.
  • Operated the dynamometer to measure the performance of the primary motor under various load conditions.
  • Adjusted the load on the secondary motor to simulate different operating conditions for the primary motor.
  • Tracked load motor temperatures during testing and identified thermal overheating caused by back-driven power from the primary motor.
    • Integrated a higher-capacity ESC designed to handle regenerative back-driving loads effectively.
    • Added resistors in parallel to absorb and dissipate excess electrical energy from the secondary motor.

Dynamometer Experiment

  • Added the torque cell to the set-up to measure the torque produced by the primary motor.
  • Operated the dynamometer to measure the performance of the primary motor under different loading conditions.
  • Utilized LabVIEW to record and analyze the torque cell and Odrive ESC output data.
  • Recorded the torque, armature current, and power consumption of the primary motor.
  • Conducted three trials for every possible combination of load conditions.
  • Wrote a MATLAB script to iterate through the collected data.
    • Found the average values of the torque, armature current, and power consumption for each load condition for each individual trial.
    • Plotted the relationship between the applied torque and the armature current of the primary motor.
  • Analyzed the recorded data to determine the performance characteristics of the primary motor.
  • Compared the performance results with theoretical predictions to validate the accuracy of the dynamometer setup.
  • Documented the experimental results and observations for further analysis and reporting.
